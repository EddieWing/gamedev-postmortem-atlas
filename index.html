Gamedev Postmortem Atlas

Game production is like Tetris. Good decisions and achievements disappear, while bad decisions and mistakes pile up and backfire. By the time a decision's outcome arrives, the damage is done, and it almost always traces back to pre-production. This atlas reads troubled productions backwards, using public cases collected across the past three decades. Each production fire is mapped to the pre-production decision that lit it, with real cases from AAA and indie projects. Decisions that paid off are here too. Read every case carefully and keep its context in mind.

706 decisions from 527 games, each tied to a public source.
